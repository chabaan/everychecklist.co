sections:
  - title: "8 Weeks Before"
    items:
      - "Research and get quotes from movers"
      - "Start decluttering room by room"
      - "Order packing supplies"
      - "Notify your landlord if renting"
      - "Research schools in the new area, if applicable"
      - "Start using up frozen and pantry food"
      - "Create a moving binder or shared doc"
  - title: "4 Weeks Before"
    items:
      - "Submit a change of address"
      - "Transfer or schedule utilities at both homes"
      - "Book the moving truck or freight elevator"
      - "Start packing non-essential rooms"
      - "Label every box by room and contents"
      - "Photograph electronics setups before unplugging"
      - "Arrange time off work for moving day"
  - title: "1 Week Before"
    items:
      - "Confirm moving day details with movers"
      - "Pack a first-night essentials box"
      - "Defrost and clean out the freezer"
      - "Back up important documents digitally"
      - "Charge all devices and battery packs"
      - "Confirm parking or loading zone for the truck"
  - title: "Moving Day"
    items:
      - "Do a final walkthrough of every room and closet"
      - "Take final utility meter readings"
      - "Hand over keys and openers"
      - "Check all closets, cabinets, and the garage twice"
      - "Tip movers, if that's local custom"
  - title: "After the Move"
    items:
      - "Unpack the essentials box first"
      - "Register your vehicle if you moved states"
      - "Update your address on IDs, banks, and subscriptions"
      - "Introduce yourself to neighbors"
      - "Locate the breaker box and water shutoff valve"
