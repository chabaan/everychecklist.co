readTime: 6
updated: "Aug 2026"
sections:
  - title: "6-8 Weeks Before"
    items:
      - "Confirm the date with the bride"
      - "Set a guest list with the bride's input"
      - "Choose a venue or host location"
      - "Set a budget and decide who's contributing"
      - "Pick a theme, if having one"
  - title: "2-4 Weeks Before"
    items:
      - "Send invitations"
      - "Plan the menu"
      - "Order or plan decorations"
      - "Plan games or activities"
      - "Order the cake or desserts"
  - title: "1 Week Before"
    items:
      - "Confirm final headcount"
      - "Buy remaining supplies"
      - "Prep any make-ahead food"
      - "Confirm any rentals (tables, chairs, linens)"
      - "Charge the camera or phone for photos"
  - title: "Day Before"
    items:
      - "Set up decorations if the venue allows"
      - "Prep serving dishes and utensils"
      - "Confirm timing with any vendors"
      - "Chill drinks and desserts"
  - title: "Day Of"
    items:
      - "Set up food and drink stations early"
      - "Set up a gift table"
      - "Assign someone to track gifts for thank-you notes"
      - "Queue a playlist"
      - "Take a moment to enjoy it, not just manage it"
  - title: "Games & Favors"
    items:
      - "Pick one or two games, not five"
      - "Prepare a small favor for each guest"
      - "Have a guest book or advice cards ready"
